ViralPilot

YouTube analytics and AI-assisted SEO for creators.

ViralPilot helps YouTube creators understand how their channel is performing and how to improve their videos' titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails. Paste any public video or channel link to get scores and suggestions, with no account needed. You can also sign in with Google to see private analytics for your own channel.

What ViralPilot does

  • Channel dashboard: views, uploads, posting cadence, best days to post, top keywords and best and worst performing videos, from public YouTube data.
  • Video SEO analysis: suggestions for titles, hooks, descriptions, tags, hashtags and thumbnails, plus a viral-potential checklist.
  • AI copilot: ask questions about a channel's public data.
  • Channel comparison: compare two channels side by side.
  • Your own analytics (optional sign-in): watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and demographics for the channel you sign in with, using the YouTube Analytics API (read-only).
  • Channel management (owner only): the channel owner can update their own videos' titles, descriptions, tags and playlists with ViralPilot's tools.

Why ViralPilot asks for Google access

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, ViralPilot asks for access to your YouTube account only to show your own channel's analytics and, if you choose, to update your own videos' details. ViralPilot does not sell your data, does not share it with third parties for advertising, and never changes anything on your channel unless you ask it to. See the Privacy Policy for details.
