Privacy Policy

How ViralPilot accesses, uses, stores and shares your data.

This policy explains what information ViralPilot ("the app", "we") accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and the choices you have. ViralPilot is a YouTube analytics and SEO assistant for creators.

Full policy

1Using the app without signing in

You can use ViralPilot without an account. When you look up a video or channel, the app fetches public data (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, view, like and subscriber counts) from the YouTube Data API. We do not collect your name, email or other personal information in this mode.

2Google user data we access when you sign in

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Google, or with an email address and password. Only Google sign-in gives the app access to Google user data. When you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

Permission (scope)What we accessWhy
Basic profile and email (openid, email, profile) Your name, email address and profile picture To show which account you are signed in with
View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) Your channel and its videos, including private and unlisted uploads To show your own channel's dashboard and video list
View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) Watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and audience demographics for your channel To show your channel's performance and explain why videos did or did not perform well

We only ever access the channel you signed in with. All access is read-only: ViralPilot cannot upload, delete or publish videos, post comments, or change anything on your channel.

3How we store your data

  • Account record. When you sign in (with Google or email and password), an account is created in Firebase Authentication, and a basic record is saved in Cloud Firestore (both Google services): your user ID, email address, name, profile picture URL, and when the account was created and last signed in. If you sign in with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. This record is used only to run sign-in and keep a list of registered users. It never contains YouTube or YouTube Analytics data.
  • Sign-in tokens for Google are kept in secure storage on your device and are used only to call Google's APIs for you.
  • Cached analysis results, recent lookups, pinned channels and chat history are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • We do not keep copies of your YouTube or YouTube Analytics data on any server or database.

4How we share data

We do not sell your data and do not share Google user data with third parties, except as needed to provide the features you use:

  • Google / YouTube APIs: to fetch the data described above.
  • AI providers (Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq): when you request an AI analysis or chat, the relevant video or channel details (for example titles, descriptions, tags, statistics and thumbnail image) are sent to the selected AI provider to generate suggestions. If you are signed in with Google and chatting about your own channel, a summary of your channel's YouTube Analytics for the last 28 days is also sent so the AI can answer from real data: views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, net subscribers, top traffic sources and top audience age/gender groups. This happens only when you use the AI features, and only to answer your request. Your email address, Google account credentials and sign-in tokens are never sent to AI providers.
  • Google AdMob: the mobile app shows ads. AdMob may collect device information such as advertising ID, IP address and app usage to serve and measure ads; see how Google uses information from ads. Google user data obtained through sign-in is never used for advertising.
  • Firebase (Google): Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore store your account record (see section 3); Remote Config and App Check are used for app configuration and to protect the app's API access. None of these receive your YouTube or YouTube Analytics data.

5Google API Services User Data Policy

ViralPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ViralPilot uses YouTube API Services; by using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out at any time from the app's home screen. This removes the sign-in tokens from your device.
  • Revoke ViralPilot's access at any time from your Google Account's Third-party connections page or via Google security settings.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all locally stored data.
  • To delete your account and its record, email mubarakansari715@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete them.

7Children

ViralPilot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
