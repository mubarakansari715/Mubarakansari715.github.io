ViralPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information ViralPilot ("the app", "we") accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and the choices you have. ViralPilot is a YouTube analytics and SEO assistant for creators.

1. Using the app without signing in

You can use ViralPilot without an account. When you look up a video or channel, the app fetches public data (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, view, like and subscriber counts) from the YouTube Data API. We do not collect your name, email or other personal information in this mode.

2. Google user data we access when you sign in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, the app requests these permissions:

Permission (scope)What we accessWhy
Basic profile and email (openid, email, profile) Your name, email address and profile picture To show which account you are signed in with
View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) Your channel and its videos, including private and unlisted uploads To show your own channel's dashboard and video list
View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) Watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and audience demographics for your channel To show your channel's performance and explain why videos did or did not perform well
Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl), used only by the channel-management tools Your videos' titles, descriptions, tags and playlists To update your own videos' details, only when you ask it to

We only ever access the channel you signed in with. We never upload, delete or publish videos, post comments, or change anything on your channel without your action.

3. How we store your data

  • On your device only. ViralPilot has no server or account database of its own. Sign-in tokens are kept on your device and are used only to call Google's APIs for you.
  • Cached analysis results, recent lookups, pinned channels and chat history are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • We do not keep copies of your YouTube or YouTube Analytics data on any server we control.

4. How we share data

We do not sell your data and do not share Google user data with third parties, except as needed to provide the features you use:

  • Google / YouTube APIs: to fetch the data described above.
  • AI providers (Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq): when you request an AI analysis or chat, the relevant video or channel details (for example titles, descriptions, tags, statistics and thumbnail image) are sent to the selected AI provider to generate suggestions. Your Google account credentials are never sent to AI providers.
  • Google AdMob: the mobile app shows ads. AdMob may collect device information such as advertising ID, IP address and app usage to serve and measure ads; see how Google uses information from ads. Google user data obtained through sign-in is never used for advertising.
  • Firebase (Google): used for app configuration and to protect the app's API access (Remote Config, App Check). These do not receive your YouTube data.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

ViralPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ViralPilot uses YouTube API Services; by using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out at any time from the app's home screen. This removes the sign-in tokens from your device.
  • Revoke ViralPilot's access at any time from your Google Account's Third-party connections page or via Google security settings.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all locally stored data.

7. Children

ViralPilot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
